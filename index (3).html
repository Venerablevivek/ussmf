About U.S.S.M.F.

Protecting athletes at every altitude.

The United States Sports Medicine Federation is dedicated to maintaining and promoting athlete health, preventing injuries, providing rehabilitation services, supporting research and education, and ensuring access to sports insurance and medical protection.

From elite aerobatic pilots to weekend paraglider pilots and youth drone racers, U.S.S.M.F. delivers a unified, trusted, science-backed framework that every American athlete deserves.

Global network

Allied federations & partners.

U.S.S.M.F. sits at the center of a network of air sports organizations united in advancing athlete excellence.

World Air Sports Federation

W.A.S.F. oversees independent air sports disciplines globally. U.S.S.M.F. is allied with W.A.S.F. to deliver specialized sports medicine care to elite aerial athletes worldwide.
